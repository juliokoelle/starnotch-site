What's new

Every update to the Starnotch app, newest first. The full record is the commit history on GitHub.

  1. 28 October 2026Wednesday

    A command palette, an agents inbox and Ask Tara

    • New

      ⌃⌥K opens a command palette that searches tabs, sessions, loops, today's events, tools and settings. What you pick often comes first.

    • New

      ⌃⌥A opens the agents inbox: permission prompts, questions, finished turns and failed loops, one card at a time. Beside the notch, a count shows how many are waiting.

    • New

      Ask Tara: press ⌃⌥T or click on Tara and type a question. She answers from your meetings, quick captures and notes, with sources, and follow-ups keep the thread.

    • New

      Cursor, Copilot CLI, Gemini CLI and opencode sessions show in the notch next to Claude Code and Codex, and a click jumps into the exact terminal tab. Plan limits now say when they run out at this pace.

    • New

      Connect an Obsidian vault or any Markdown folder, in onboarding or Settings: Today shows your tasks and ticks them off in the file itself. Weather, day progress and a notepad join the tools, each off until you switch it on.

    • Improved

      Now playing works with most players and can show synced lyrics from LRCLIB. Like dictation and tools, it opens in a smaller panel, and Tara wears headphones while something plays.

  2. 27 October 2026Tuesday

    A calm first run and a System tab

    • New

      The first launch is one window in four steps: meet Tara and pick her look, set up your agents in one click, choose how Tara thinks, switch on the tools you want. Every step can be skipped.

    • Improved

      On a fresh install, every tool except Agents, Calendar and Now playing starts off. Nothing runs until you switch it on, including tools a later version adds.

    • New

      The System tab shows CPU, GPU, memory and network speed beside your top apps, with switches for keep awake, dark mode and hiding the desktop icons.

    • New

      Keep awake now also works with the lid closed, on the power adapter, for 1 h, 3 h or until you unplug. A ring beside the notch shows the time left.

    • Improved

      Now playing shows a large cover, a scrubber to seek, and the volume with the output device by name.

    • Improved

      The open island no longer cuts a menu bar icon in half. With Accessibility allowed, it ends between two icons.

  3. 26 October 2026Monday

    Calendar views, everyday tools and agents that ask

    • New

      The calendar shows your Day, Week or Month in the notch.

    • New

      Tools beside the notch: a countdown, a stopwatch and a focus timer, clipboard history on ⌃⌥V, and a file shelf. Drag files onto the closed notch and it opens on the shelf.

    • New

      Agents can ask with options to pick or a field to type. Through the MCP server, any agent can show a line in the notch or ask you yes or no.

    • New

      The agents panel lists recent sessions, resumed with one click, and loops from any recurring agent that writes one line per run to a log.

    • New

      AirPods battery shows in the notch when they connect.

    • New

      Watch mode: while a video plays fullscreen, the island hides completely.

  4. 24 October 2026Saturday

    First release: your agents in the notch

    • New

      Starnotch turns the MacBook notch into a small island. Your Claude Code and Codex sessions show as small characters: working, waiting for you, or idle.

    • New

      Answer permission prompts from the notch: Allow once with ↵, Allow for session with ⌘↵, Deny with esc. Nothing is allowed without your click.

    • New

      Your next meeting with a countdown and Join, what is playing, and quick capture on ⌃⌥N for one line out of the notch.

    • New

      The meeting agent listens along during a call and shows contradictions, open questions and new commitments as cards, each with its source. A demo meeting shows it without a microphone.

    • New

      Claude Code and Codex plan limits side by side in the header, tabs you reorder by dragging, and ⌃⌥I opens your quick capture inbox from anywhere.

That is everything since the first release. Smaller changes are in the commit history.
