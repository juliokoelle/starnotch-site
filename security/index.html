Security

Starnotch keeps what it knows about you on your Mac. This page shows where your data lives, when something leaves it, and what stays in your hands.

We never train models on user data.

Your Mac

Tara's memory is stored on your Mac, in the app's own folder: the meetings, notes, quick captures and other sources you allow. The notch and the dashboard read it there. Starnotch has no account and no telemetry, so the app sends nothing to us.

The app reads your coding agents' session files read-only and never reads your Keychain or browser cookies. Permission prompts from your agents reach the app over a local connection that only processes of your own user can use.

When Tara answers

Tara thinks with the AI you choose in Settings. When she answers, your question and the excerpts from her memory she needs for that answer go to that provider:

  • your Claude plan, through Claude Code (Anthropic)
  • your own Anthropic API key (Anthropic)
  • your ChatGPT plan, through Codex (OpenAI)
  • Apple's on-device model, in which case nothing leaves your Mac

The provider handles what it receives under your account and its terms. More on this in AI at Starnotch.

Meetings

Tara can import meetings from Jamie, Granola and Wispr Flow, using your own accounts there. What she imports is kept in her memory on your Mac.

Live transcription during a meeting uses Reson8, and only if you add your own Reson8 key. While it runs, the meeting's audio goes to Reson8 under your account. Make sure everyone in the meeting agrees to being transcribed.

Your controls

In Settings › Tara's memory you can:

  • pause her memory at any time,
  • switch single sources off,
  • delete what she remembers, source by source.

Tara's memory in your other AI apps

If you choose, AI apps such as Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor and ChatGPT can read Tara's memory through a server that runs on your Mac (MCP). It is read-only and leaves out every source you switched off. What such an app looks up goes to its own AI provider with your chat.

Other services the app can ask

A few tools ask a web service, and only once you switch them on: synced lyrics ask LRCLIB, the weather asks Open-Meteo, and time to leave asks Apple Maps.

Our server: the waitlist only

The only Starnotch server holds the waitlist: the names and email addresses entered on starnotch.app, stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany. The website itself is a set of static pages on GitHub Pages, and visits are counted with Umami, without cookies. The full list is on Subprocessors, the details in the privacy policy.

Open source

The core of Starnotch is open source under the Apache License 2.0, so anyone can read what the app does: github.com/juliokoelle/starnotch. Tara and the Cat are all rights reserved.
