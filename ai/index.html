AI at Starnotch

Where Starnotch uses AI, which model does the work, and what it gets to see.

We never train models on user data.

Where AI is used

  • Ask Tara. She answers your questions from her memory and shows the sources she used.
  • Background summaries. Tara writes short summaries, such as your work blocks and profiles of the people in your notes.
  • Meeting analysis. During a meeting you transcribe live, the transcript and your meeting memory go to the AI you chose.

The background summaries can use a different one of these than Ask Tara. Your provider handles what it receives under your account and its terms, including whether it may use it for training.

What is sent

When Tara answers, she sends your question and the excerpts from her memory she needs for that answer. If you drop her on a window, a picture of that window goes along. Her memory itself stays on your Mac. The details are on Security.

Training

We never train models on user data. The app sends us nothing, so we have nothing to train on.

Transcription

Live meeting transcription uses Reson8, and only if you add your own Reson8 key. Meetings you import from Jamie, Granola or Wispr Flow come from your own accounts there.

What AI never does

  • Approve for you. No model allows anything your coding agents ask for. Only your click or key does; without an answer, the agent asks in its own window as usual.
  • Pretend to be a person. Tara is a character. When she answers, the words come from the model you chose.
